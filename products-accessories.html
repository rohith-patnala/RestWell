Care guide

Make your mattress last longer

  • Protector — always use one. It's the cheapest way to extend mattress life.
  • Topper — adds 2–3 years to a sagging mattress and changes firmness.
  • Rotate — rotate your mattress head-to-toe every 3 months.
  • Air out — strip the bed weekly and let the mattress breathe for an hour.
