Trial & Returns

How does the 100-night trial work?

You get 100 nights from the delivery date to sleep on your mattress. If it's not right, request a return in your account and we'll pick it up for free — full refund within 7 days.

Can I return during the trial?

Yes, anytime within 100 nights. No questions asked, no restocking fee. Pickup is free across India.

How long does a refund take?

Refunds are processed within 7 days of pickup. The exact time depends on your bank, usually 3–5 business days.

Delivery

Is delivery really free?

Yes — free across India on every order, including mattresses, beds, and accessories.

How long does delivery take?

Mattresses and accessories ship in 3–5 days. Beds and made-to-order items take 7–10 days.

Do you offer installation?

Yes, free white-glove setup for mattresses and free assembly for beds, by our trained team.

Warranty

How long is the warranty?

Mattresses carry a 10-year warranty (15 years on Luxe models). Beds have a 5-year warranty. Pillows and accessories have 2–5 year warranties.

What does the warranty cover?

Manufacturing defects and sagging deeper than 1.5 inches under normal use. It doesn't cover normal wear, stains, or misuse.

Care

How do I clean my mattress?

Spot-clean with mild detergent and cold water. Always use a waterproof protector. We offer an annual deep-clean service with Care Plus.

How often should I rotate my mattress?

Rotate head-to-toe every 3 months for the first year, then every 6 months. Our mattresses are single-sided — no flipping needed.

Our sleep experts are here Mon–Sat, 9am–8pm.
