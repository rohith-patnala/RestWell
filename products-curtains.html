Measuring guide

Measure once, fit perfectly

  • Width — measure the rod, not the window. Add 20% for fullness.
  • Length — from rod to 1 inch above the floor for a clean look.
  • Fullness — curtain width should be 2x the rod width.
  • Custom — order made-to-measure for odd sizes, free hemming.
