Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Contents1. Information we collect2. How we use it3. Who we share with4. How we store it5. Your rights6. Contact

This policy explains what information RestWell collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our website or app, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We collect information you give us directly — like your name, email, phone, and delivery address when you place an order or create an account. We also collect information automatically, like pages visited, device type, and approximate location.

From your mattress (optional)

If you use a RestWell smart mattress, it collects sleep data (movement, heart rate, breathing). This stays on-device unless you opt in to cloud sync.

2. How we use it

  • To process and deliver your orders
  • To provide customer support and trial returns
  • To send order updates and (if you opt in) marketing emails
  • To improve our products and website
  • To prevent fraud and abuse

3. Who we share with

We share data only with service providers who help us operate — delivery partners, payment processors, and email tools. They're bound by contract to use your data only for our service, never to sell it.

We never sell your personal data.

4. How we store it

Your data is stored on secure servers in India. We keep order data for 7 years (as required by tax law) and account data until you ask us to delete it. Sleep data on-device is kept until you reset your mattress.

5. Your rights

  • Access — request a copy of your data
  • Correction — fix anything that's wrong
  • Deletion — ask us to delete your account
  • Opt-out — unsubscribe from marketing anytime

Email privacy@restwell.in to exercise any of these rights.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy? Write to our Data Protection Officer at privacy@restwell.in or RestWell Pvt Ltd, Indiranagar, Bengaluru 560038.
