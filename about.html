Our story

Crafting better sleep since 2014

From a small Jaipur workshop to 2 lakh bedrooms across India — every mattress is still made by hand, then tested by sleep experts.

How it began

A grandfather's bad back started it all

In 2014, our founder Aditya watched his grandfather struggle with sleep night after night. Every mattress he tried was either too firm or too soft — never quite right. So Aditya set out to build one that adapted to the body, not the other way around.

Today, RestWell mattresses are crafted with 7-zone orthopedic foam, certified safe foams, and a cooling cover designed for Indian summers — all backed by a 100-night trial.
